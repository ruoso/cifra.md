# Capo on the Third Fret
- notation: american

The chart names the harmony as it sounds. The first block is a guitar with
a capo at the third fret, which is just a tuning three semitones up: its
shapes are the E, A and B7 shapes, and a reader may say so beside each
chord. The ukulele block voices the same chords in its own tuning.
